Industrial Error Analysis & Engineering Insights

Missing Hole Perfect Precision & Recall (99.5% AP)

Missing hole defects exhibit circular geometry and distinct background contrast against copper terminal pads, allowing the P3 feature maps to achieve 100% precision and 100% recall with zero misses.

Short Circuit High Detection (99.5% AP)

Short circuit bridging between traces was effectively captured with 97.4% precision and 100% recall thanks to Albumentations blur and mixup (0.3) augmentation preventing trace boundary blurring.

Spur & Mouse Bite Edge Variation (97.3% – 98.3% AP)

Extended 100-epoch training and disabling mosaic at epoch 90 resolved earlier micro-notch classification errors, boosting Mouse Bite from 83.7% to 98.3% AP and Spur from 84.2% to 97.3% AP.

The 6 DeepPCB Defect Categories in Industrial Electronics

Domain Knowledge & Defect Mechanisms

1. Missing Hole (missing_hole)

Un-drilled through-hole or missing via pad preventing pin insertion or layer-to-layer electrical connectivity.

Test Instances: 107 | AP@50: 99.5% | Precision: 100% | Recall: 100%

2. Short Circuit (short)

Accidental conductive copper bridging connecting two separate signal tracks. Causes component burnout and system short.

Test Instances: 113 | AP@50: 99.5% | Precision: 97.4% | Recall: 100%

3. Spurious Copper (spurious_copper)

Residual isolated copper island or etching artifact floating in non-conductive clearance zones due to incomplete chemical etching.

Test Instances: 134 | AP@50: 97.9% | Precision: 99.3% | Recall: 99.3%

4. Open Circuit (open_circuit)

Complete fracture or break in conductive copper trace interrupting signal transmission and current continuity.

Test Instances: 143 | AP@50: 98.1% | Precision: 99.3% | Recall: 97.2%

5. Spur (spur)

Sharp metallic protrusion or horn projecting outwards from a conductor edge. Can trigger dielectric breakdown and crosstalk.

Test Instances: 107 | AP@50: 97.3% | Precision: 100% | Recall: 97.2%

6. Mouse Bite (mouse_bite)

Small scallop notch or erosion on conductor trace edge reducing effective current-carrying cross-section and impedance.

Test Instances: 94 | AP@50: 98.3% | Precision: 96.9% | Recall: 98.9%

YOLOv8s Neural Network Pipeline

Input: 640×640×3 single PCB optical inspection image.

1. Modified CSPDarknet Backbone
Extracts hierarchical multi-scale visual features via Conv and C2f cross-stage partial bottleneck blocks. Concludes with Spatial Pyramid Pooling Fast (SPPF).
2. Path Aggregation Network (PANet) Neck
Combines high-level semantic abstractions with low-level spatial detail via top-down and bottom-up feature pyramids at P3 (80×80), P4 (40×40), and P5 (20×20).
3. Decoupled Anchor-Free Detect Head
Separates classification and bounding-box regression tasks. Uses Distribution Focal Loss (DFL) and CIoU loss for sub-pixel boundary refinement.

Minor Project Viva & Oral Examination Prep Sheet

Top Questions & Verified Technical Answers

Q1: Why choose YOLOv8s over conventional two-stage detectors like Faster R-CNN for PCB defect inspection?

Answer: Two-stage detectors (Faster R-CNN) run at only 5–15 FPS due to region proposal network latency, making them impractical for high-speed automated optical inspection (AOI) conveyor lines. YOLOv8s is a single-stage, anchor-free detector achieving 12.4 ms inference latency (~80 FPS) on Tesla T4 while achieving 98.40% mAP@50, 96.82% Precision, and 96.47% Recall across all 6 industrial defect classes.

Q2: How did you handle micro-defect detection on high-density circuit boards?

Answer: DeepPCB defects occupy less than 0.5% of the overall image area. To prevent spatial resolution degradation: (1) Maintained native 640×640 input resolution, (2) Selected YOLOv8s which maintains high-capacity P3 feature maps (80×80) in the PANet neck, (3) Leveraged Mixup augmentation (0.3) alongside Blur and MedianBlur to help the model learn complex topological overlaps.

Q3: Which defect classes performed best and why?

Answer: Missing Hole and Short Circuit achieved near-perfect performance with 99.5% AP@50 (100% Recall). After 100-epoch convergence, previously challenging micro-edge defects also surged: Mouse Bite achieved 98.3% AP@50, Open Circuit reached 98.1% AP@50, Spurious Copper achieved 97.9% AP@50, and Spur reached 97.3% AP@50.

Q4: How did you prevent overfitting during extended 100-epoch training?

Answer: We applied 3-Fold Cross-Validation, AdamW weight decay (0.0005), and cosine learning rate decay (`lr0=0.001`, `lrf=0.0001`). At epoch 90, mosaic augmentation was disabled (`close_mosaic=10`) to allow fine-tuning on natural PCB topologies without overfitting. Validation class loss smoothly decreased to 0.67, confirming true generalization.

Q5: How can this system be deployed on actual industrial factory lines?

Answer: In our pipeline, the model can be exported to ONNX (opset 12) and accelerated via NVIDIA TensorRT on edge devices like NVIDIA Jetson Orin or Intel OpenVINO on industrial PCs, enabling 120+ FPS continuous conveyor inspection with automated PASS/REJECT triggers.
